*** What is conditional probability? :version:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:           019ba6c8-6504-70a5-8b16-e5753108653b
:ROAM_EXCLUDE: t
:END:
For events $A$ and $B$ with $P(B)>0$,
$P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)$.

**** Notes :notes:
Conditioning restricts the sample space to $B$.

*** How does conditioning change a sample space? :version:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:           019ba6c9-a918-774e-9486-bb9e44fdc64e
:ROAM_EXCLUDE: t
:END:
It restricts possible outcomes to the conditioning event and renormalizes them.
